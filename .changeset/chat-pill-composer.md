Chat composer is now a one-line pill that grows with the text (up to about 40% of the screen, then scrolls): ＋ (Attach file, Save as draft, Drafts, Enter sends), the text, 🎤 and the send button. While the agent runs the send button becomes ■ Stop, and with text in the pill ➤ steers with ■ Stop beside it. A small context ring with its percentage, "Cancel queued (N)" and "Drafts (N)" sit above the pill; the settings strip no longer holds the context label, Cancel queued or Abort (except on a read-only session, which has no composer). Expand / Collapse and the "Press Enter to send" row are gone. The conversation stays scrolled to the latest message when the pill grows or the phone keyboard opens.
