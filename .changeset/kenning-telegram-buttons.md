Blocking kenning questions are now easy to answer from Telegram. The message says how ("tap a button below, or swipe this message to reply"), and a question with fixed options (yes / no) shows them as buttons: one tap answers it, the buttons disappear and the bot confirms. Before, only a Telegram reply to the question answered it, and a plain "yes" typed in the chat went to the assistant instead, leaving the question open.
