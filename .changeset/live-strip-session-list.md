The session list now has a "Live" strip under the filter box: every session that is running, waiting for you or waiting on another session, across all folders, ordered by how much it needs you. Each row shows its role or name, folder, a state badge, its status line and its owner, opens like a folder row, and the strip is collapsible (remembered on the device), capped at 6 rows with "+N more", follows the filter box, and hides when nothing is live. Working sessions show as running even without shipmates.
