Phone: swipe an open session row left to reveal "⊘ Close". Releasing past the button closes the session through the usual close flow (owned-session prompt, Undo banner); releasing a little short of it leaves the button showing to tap; a short swipe snaps back. Only one row shows the button at a time. Vertical scrolling, a tap on a row (which still opens it) and a tap on the strip right of the drawer are unchanged, and closed rows and read-only rows do not swipe.
