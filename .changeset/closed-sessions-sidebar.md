Close and reopen sessions from the sidebar and the top bar. Each session row gets a Close button (beside star and delete) and the top bar a close/reopen toggle for the open session. Closed sessions are hidden from the folder list and its filter unless the new "Show closed" toggle is on (remembered on this device), in which case they show dimmed with a Reopen action. A folder whose sessions are all closed disappears, owned-session chips hide closed children, a closed parent of an open fork stays as dimmed context, and a working closed session still appears in the Live strip.
