Needs you: long questions are readable. The panel stays compact, each question clamped to three lines at a slightly larger size, and a tap on a question (or "Read ⤢") opens it in a reader: a modal up to 900px wide on desktop, a full-screen sheet on the phone. The reader shows the question at 16px with its Markdown rendered (lists, code, links), every option as a full-width button with its whole text, a multi-line answer box, "Open session ›", and ‹ n of N › to step through the other questions without closing. Keys 1 to 9 pick an option, Ctrl/Cmd+Enter sends a typed answer, Escape closes, and it closes by itself when the question is answered anywhere. A long question (or one with long options) is answered from the reader; a short one keeps its option chips and answer field in the panel. The question toast's "Answer in Needs you" now opens the reader on that question.
