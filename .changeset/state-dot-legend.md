Session list: one state-dot legend for every session row and By-owner group summary. Amber (with a halo) now only means "waiting for you"; purple is waiting on another session, blue and pulsing is running, green is loaded but idle, grey is not loaded, and a hollow ring is closed (a live closed session shows its live colour). Hovering a dot, or long-pressing it on a touch screen, shows its label, which is also its accessible name. A collapsed By-owner group shows its live counts as coloured dots next to the session count.
