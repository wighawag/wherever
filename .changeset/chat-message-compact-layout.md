Chat messages get a compact layout: assistant replies render full width with no bubble, your own messages are compact right-aligned bubbles, and each generic tool call is a one-line chip (status, tool name, short args, duration) that expands to its arguments and output on tap, keeping the download link, inline image / audio / video previews and the auto-expanded `!command`. On desktop the messages and the composer share a centered column of about 760px. Copy, Raw / Rendered and Fork now sit in an action row under the message, shown on hover or focus and pinned open by a tap on touch screens.
