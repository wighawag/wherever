Voice input settings (engine, accent / locale) now live in one shared store and the Speech Settings panel is its own component, so it can be opened from anywhere and a change applies to the 🎤 button at once (existing saved settings are kept). A speech error now also shows next to 🎤 in the composer pill, with a × to dismiss it, instead of only inside the settings panel.
